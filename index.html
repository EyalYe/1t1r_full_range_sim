RERGON · IHP SG13G2 130 nm · 64×64 1T1R RRAM PiM macro · pre-silicon

One 8×8 slice, read in the time domain

Each column precharges its bit line, opens the selected word lines and discharges through the cells. An 8-tap delay line samples the buffered bit line (BLD), and the count of taps that still saw BL high is the column's thermometer code. Opening 8 rows at once turns that read into NOR, majority or popcount.

Where the numbers come from
  • Crossing times of the buffer threshold (≈0.6 V) and the resulting codes follow the paper's transistor-level results with extracted line capacitance and CMIM = 10 fF (Fig. 4, Table I). LRS is 9 kΩ.
  • 8-row, HRS 1 MΩ (ideal case), Vdrain/Vgate/VDEL = 1.0/0.8/0.8 V: BL crosses at 39.7 ns for k = 0 and 29.6 ns for k = 1, compressing to 22.1 ns at k = 8. At +10 ns the codes are 8 8 8 7 4 2 0 0 0; at +20 ns NOR is full scale.
  • 8-row, HRS 200 kΩ, 0.8/1.0/0.8 V: the whole band is 13.3–14.1 ns, so NOR and majority are not resolved and only 3 popcount codes appear.
  • Single-row read, 0.8/0.8/0.8 V: LRS crosses at 28 ns, a 200 kΩ HRS at about 51 ns; a window at +20 or +30 ns separates them by all 8 taps. The paper does not report a single-row 1 MΩ crossing; the demo only shows it as later than the plot.
  • Tap positions (≈0.31 ns pitch at VDEL = 0.8 V) are fitted so the model reproduces those codes. The waveform shape between precharge and threshold is drawn as a simple exponential for illustration; only the crossing times are from simulation.
  • Writes: TS_LS with DIN = 1 drives SL to Vdrain and BL to ground (SET → LRS); DIN = 0 drives BL to Vdrain and SL to ground (RESET → HRS). Reads use the RESET polarity at ≤0.18 V and ≤2.6 µA per cell.
  • The full macro has 64 columns, so one access returns 64 × 8 = 512 bits. This page shows one 8×8 block, the same block the paper simulates.
